FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full detail take?+
A full service wash typically takes 45–90 minutes. A premium detail can take 3–5 hours depending on the vehicle size and condition. We always provide a time estimate before we begin.
Do you offer mobile / on-site carwash?+
Yes! We offer mobile detailing for premium and fleet packages. Our team will come to your home, office, or any location that suits you. Contact us for availability and pricing.
What products do you use?+
We use eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning products and pH-neutral shampoos that are tough on dirt but gentle on your vehicle's paint, trim, and environment.
Are you insured?+
Absolutely. ABJ Carwash carries full liability insurance and all team members are bonded. Every staff member passes a thorough background check before joining our team.
Can I reschedule or cancel a booking?+
Yes. We ask for at least 24 hours notice for rescheduling or cancellations at no charge. Changes made within 24 hours may incur a small fee. You can reschedule by calling us or via WhatsApp.
What if I'm not satisfied with the result?+
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your service, contact us within 24 hours and we'll re-clean your vehicle at no additional cost.
